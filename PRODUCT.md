# Portfolio Product Direction

## Register
Brand portfolio.

## Platform
Web-only portfolio, designed desktop-first and responsive down to mobile.

## Audience
Hiring teams, founders or clients, and engineers evaluating practical product judgment.

## Purpose
Present Faizan Karamat as a software engineer who can ship polished full-stack products and practical AI workflows. The site should make the work easy to inspect, make the resume easy to download, and make contacting Faizan the obvious next step.

## Positioning
A product-minded engineer for polished web systems, AI-enabled workflows, dashboards, and backend-heavy product builds.

## Conversion
Primary action: contact Faizan.

Secondary action: download resume.

Tertiary action: view selected projects.

## Belief Ladder
Visitors should leave believing:

1. Faizan has strong full-stack engineering fundamentals.
2. He can apply AI in useful, product-facing ways rather than as decoration.
3. His work has taste, restraint, and practical delivery quality.
4. He is worth contacting for engineering roles or product builds.

## Proof
Use project screenshots, concise case-study descriptions, GitHub links, and the attached resumes. Avoid inflated claims without visible work behind them.

## Personality
Precise, cinematic, practical.

## Anti-References
Avoid AI-generated slop, generic template-portfolio sections, decorative background lines or grids, fake agency language, loud gradients, and resume text that reads like filler.

## Accessibility
Use readable contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, clear focus states, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain coherent on mobile.
